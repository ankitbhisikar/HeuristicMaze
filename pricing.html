Frequently Asked Questions

How does plan selection work with the backend?
When you select or upgrade a plan, your choice is sent via POST /api/pricing/subscribe directly to the local SQLite database (beacon.db). The users table updates your subscription tier immediately.
Can I test the A* Algorithm and Map Pathfinder on the Free tier?
Yes! The Free plan gives you full access to both the interactive Maze Grid with Character Animation and the City Map Shortest Route Pathfinder.
